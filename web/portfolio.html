What I build

Four things, mostly.

If your problem is not one of these, say so anyway. I will tell you honestly whether it is worth automating.

Support

Answers from your documents

Your policies, your prices, your delivery times. When the answer is not in your documents the assistant says so and hands the question to a person, instead of inventing one.

Sales

Qualification and routing

Every conversation gets a score. The ones worth money reach your CRM, Slack or an inbox within seconds, with the full transcript attached so nobody asks the customer twice.

Operations

Orders into spreadsheets, invoices into accounting, forms into the CRM. Built in n8n so you can see the whole flow on one screen and change it without me.

Data

Honestly

When not to hire me.

  • Your process is not written down and nobody agrees on how it works. Automation does not fix a broken process — it makes it break faster and more quietly. Write it down first. Then this is a short job.
  • You want a bot that improvises and sounds human. Mine are built to say “I do not know that, let me get someone who does.” I think that is the right default for a business, but if you want the opposite we will both be unhappy.
  • You need a mobile app, a brand identity, or someone to run ads. I do not do those and I will not learn them on your budget.
  • The whole job is one afternoon of clicking. If a free tier solves it, use the free tier. Tell me and I will point you at it.
