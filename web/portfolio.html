A build you can open right now

Not a screenshot. A running system with its source code public.

AI Support & Lead Agent. A chat agent for an e-commerce brand. It answers questions from the company's own knowledge base, shows which section each answer came from, watches for buying intent, and pushes qualified leads into n8n — which fans them out to Slack, a CRM, a spreadsheet and a follow-up email.

The part most people miss: leads are written to the database before the webhook fires. If n8n is down the lead is not lost, and one button replays every undelivered one. That is the difference between a demo and something you can leave running.

When not to hire me

  • Your process is not written down and nobody agrees on how it works. Automation does not fix a broken process, it makes it break faster and more quietly. Write it down first. Then it is a short job.
  • You want a bot that improvises and sounds human. Mine are built to say “I do not know that, let me get someone who does.” I think that is the right default for a business, but if you want the opposite we will both be unhappy.
  • You need a mobile app, a brand identity, or someone to run ads. I do not do those and I do not want to learn them on your budget.
  • The whole job is one afternoon of clicking. If Zapier's free tier solves it, use Zapier's free tier. Tell me and I will point you at it.

Tell me what is eating the time.

One paragraph is enough: what the repetitive task is, roughly how often it happens, and which tools it touches. I reply within a business day, and I will tell you if it is not worth automating.
