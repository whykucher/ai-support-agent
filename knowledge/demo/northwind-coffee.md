# About Northwind Coffee Co.

Northwind Coffee Co. is a specialty coffee roaster based in Portland, Oregon. We
roast to order five days a week and ship within 24 hours of roasting. We sell
direct to consumers through our online store and to cafes, offices and hotels
through our wholesale program. Founded in 2016, we work with 14 producing
partners across Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala and Indonesia.

# Shipping and delivery

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Pacific ship the same business day. Standard
shipping inside the United States is 3 to 5 business days and costs $5.90.
Orders over $45 ship free. Express shipping is 2 business days for $14.50.

International shipping is available to Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany and
Australia. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and starts at
$18.00. Customs duties are the responsibility of the recipient.

Every order gets a tracking number by email as soon as the label is printed. If
tracking has not moved for 4 business days, contact support and we will open a
carrier claim and reship at no cost.

We do not ship on weekends or US federal holidays.

# Returns and refunds

Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Open
bags are covered by our freshness guarantee: if you are unhappy with the coffee
for any reason within 14 days, we refund or replace it, and you do not need to
send the bag back.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days
to appear. Subscription orders can be refunded up to 24 hours after they are
charged, before the coffee is roasted.

Damaged or missing items must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Send a
photo to support and we ship a replacement immediately, no return required.

# Pricing and products

Single-origin 12 oz bags are $18.50. Blends are $16.00 for 12 oz. The 5 lb bulk
bag of any blend is $78.00. Instant coffee sticks are $14.00 for a box of 10.

Our current lineup:
- Harbor Blend - chocolate, hazelnut, medium roast, our best seller
- Cascade Dark - dark roast, molasses and toasted almond
- Ethiopia Yirgacheffe - single origin, blueberry and jasmine, light roast
- Colombia Huila - single origin, caramel and red apple, medium roast
- Decaf Sunset - Swiss water process decaf, medium roast

Grind options are whole bean, drip, espresso, French press and Aeropress. Whole
bean is recommended because ground coffee loses aroma within about a week.

# Subscriptions

Subscriptions ship every 1, 2 or 4 weeks and save 15% off the retail price.
There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee. You can pause, skip, change
the coffee or change the frequency any time from your account page.

Subscription charges happen 48 hours before each shipment. Changing the coffee
after the charge is not possible because the roast is already scheduled, but
support can apply a credit to the next order.

The first subscription box includes a free 4 oz sample of a rotating micro-lot.

# Wholesale and B2B

Our wholesale program serves cafes, restaurants, offices and hotels. Wholesale
pricing starts at 30% off retail for orders of 20 lb per month and reaches 45%
off at 200 lb per month. Wholesale accounts get net 30 payment terms after the
first three orders.

The wholesale onboarding process is: intake call, free 3 lb sample kit, pricing
proposal, then a standing delivery schedule. Typical turnaround from first call
to first delivery is 10 business days.

We provide free equipment loans (grinders and brewers) for accounts ordering
40 lb or more per month. We also run free barista training for cafe partners,
either on site in the Portland metro area or over video.

Office coffee service includes a monthly delivery, equipment maintenance and a
consumption dashboard. Office plans start at $180 per month for up to 25 people.

Private label roasting is available from 500 lb per order, including bag design
and printing.

# Brewing help

For drip, use 60 grams of coffee per litre of water at 93 to 96 degrees Celsius.
For espresso, start with an 18 gram dose, a 36 gram yield, in 27 to 30 seconds.
For French press, use a coarse grind, a 4 minute steep, and press slowly.

Store coffee in an airtight container at room temperature, away from light. Do
not freeze coffee you are actively drinking; freezing is only useful for long
term storage of unopened bags.

Coffee is best between 5 and 21 days after the roast date printed on the bag.
