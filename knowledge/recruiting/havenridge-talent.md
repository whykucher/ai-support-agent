# About Havenridge Talent

Havenridge Talent is a recruitment agency placing software engineers, data and
product people. Seven consultants. We work permanent and contract, mostly in the
UK and remote-EU roles.

We placed 143 people last year. Average time from brief to signed offer was 31
days for permanent roles.

# Fees: what we charge companies to hire

Our fee for a permanent placement is 18% of first-year base salary, invoiced
on the candidate's first day and payable in 30 days.

Retained search is 22%, split into three: on brief, on shortlist, on start. We
recommend it above about £90k, where the search is genuinely harder.

Contract placement is a margin on the day rate, typically 12% to 18% depending
on volume and length. The margin is disclosed; you always see what the
contractor gets and what we take.

Exclusivity is not required. If you also brief other agencies, tell us, and we
will still work the role properly.

So a £70,000 hire costs £12,600 at the standard rate. That price is the whole
cost; there is no advertising charge, no retainer on contingent roles and no
fee for candidates we send who you do not hire.

You can cancel a search at any time on a contingent basis and owe nothing. On
retained search the stages already invoiced stand, and anything not yet reached
is cancelled.

# Replacement guarantee

If someone we placed leaves or is dismissed within twelve weeks, we replace
them free. If we cannot replace them within eight weeks of being told, we refund
the fee on a sliding scale.

The guarantee needs the role to be substantially the same. It does not cover
redundancy or a change of scope after hiring.

# How the process runs

Day one is the brief: a 45-minute call covering the actual work, the team, the
salary band and what a bad hire in this seat would look like.

You get a longlist inside five working days and a shortlist of three to five
inside ten. Every shortlisted candidate comes with a written summary, salary
expectation, notice period and their reason for moving.

We do not send unvetted CVs in bulk. If you get five CVs from us, we have spoken
to all five.

# For candidates

Applying is free and always will be. We never charge candidates for anything.

Send a CV to apply@havenridge.example or apply to a specific role. Every
application gets a reply within three working days, including the rejections.

We will not send your CV anywhere without asking you first, by name, for that
specific company. If an agency has ever done that to you, you will understand
why we say it explicitly.

Salary expectations stay confidential from the employer until you decide to
share them.

# Sectors and roles we cover

Backend, frontend, mobile, platform and SRE. Data engineering, analytics and
machine learning. Product management and product design.

Seniority from mid-level to director. We do not do graduate volume hiring, and
we do not place C-level.

We do not cover sales, marketing, finance or general operations. Asking us to
would get you a worse result than asking a specialist.

# Contract and IR35

For contract roles inside IR35 we work through an umbrella or your own PSC via a
compliant arrangement, and the determination is the client's to make. We will
not pretend a role is outside IR35 because it is easier to fill.

Typical contract lengths are three to twelve months. Extensions are common;
about 60% of our contracts extend at least once.

Payment terms for contractors are weekly, on self-billing, paid the Friday after
the timesheet is approved.

# What slows a hire down

Salary bands set below market and not revised. Four-stage interview processes
with a week between stages. Feedback that takes more than 48 hours.

We will tell you when one of these is the reason you are losing candidates, even
when it is not what you want to hear, because the alternative is billing you for
a search that was never going to close.
