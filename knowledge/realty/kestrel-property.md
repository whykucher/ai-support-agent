# About Kestrel Property

Kestrel Property is an independent estate agency covering Bristol and the
surrounding villages. Four negotiators, one lettings manager. We handle around
25 sales and 40 tenancies a month.

Office hours are Monday to Friday 09:00 to 18:00 and Saturday 09:30 to 16:00.
Viewings run until 19:30 on weekdays because most people work.

# Selling with us: commission and fees

Our commission, or sole agency fee, is 1.2% of the sale price plus VAT, payable on completion.
Nothing is charged up front and nothing is charged if the property does not
sell. Multi agency commission is 1.8% plus VAT.

The fee covers professional photography, a floor plan, an EPC if you do not have
a valid one, listings on Rightmove and Zoopla, accompanied viewings and
negotiation through to completion.

A free valuation takes about 45 minutes at the property. We will tell you what
we think it is worth and what we think it will actually sell for, which are not
always the same number.

Average time from listing to agreed sale across our stock last year was 34 days.
Average from agreed sale to completion was 14 weeks, which is conveyancing, not
us.

# Buying

Registering as a buyer is free. Tell us your budget, the areas you want and
your position, and we will send matching properties before they go live on the
portals.

To offer on a property we need proof of funds, a mortgage agreement in principle
if you are borrowing, and your solicitor's details. Offers without these are
harder for a seller to take seriously.

We do not charge buyers anything at any point.

# Lettings

Landlord management is 10% of monthly rent plus VAT for full management, or a
one-off 60% of the first month's rent for tenant-find only.

Full management covers marketing, referencing, the tenancy agreement, deposit
protection, rent collection, arrears chasing, quarterly inspections and
arranging repairs.

Tenant referencing costs the landlord £48 per applicant. We do not charge
tenants any fees beyond the deposit and first month's rent, as required by the
Tenant Fees Act.

Deposits are five weeks' rent, held in the Deposit Protection Service.

# Viewings

Viewings are accompanied by a negotiator. We can usually offer a slot within 48
hours, including evenings and Saturdays.

We ask for 24 hours' notice to cancel. Occupied properties mean somebody has
tidied their house and left for an hour.

Video viewings are available for out-of-area buyers, done live so you can ask
questions rather than watching a recording.

# What we need from a seller

Proof of ownership and ID, an EPC, any FENSA or building regulation
certificates, the boiler service record, and details of the lease if the
property is leasehold.

Getting the leasehold pack ordered early saves weeks. It is the single most
common reason a sale drags.

# The local market

Our patch runs from Clifton and Redland through Bishopston to Horfield, plus the
villages north towards Almondsbury. Average sale price across our stock last
year was £412,000.

Two-bed flats in Redland are moving fastest. Four-bed family houses near good
catchment schools attract the most viewings but take longer to complete because
of chains.
