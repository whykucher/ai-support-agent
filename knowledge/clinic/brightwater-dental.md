# About Brightwater Dental

Brightwater Dental is a five-chair practice in Leeds with three dentists and two
hygienists. We see about 340 patients a month, roughly a third of them new.

We are open Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00, and Saturday 09:00 to 13:00 for
emergencies and hygiene appointments only.

# Booking an appointment

New patient examinations are booked online or by phone and usually have
availability within five working days. Hygiene appointments book about two weeks
ahead, so it is worth booking your next one as you leave.

You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside
24 hours we charge a £30 late-cancellation fee, because the chair time cannot be
filled at that notice.

If you are in pain we keep two emergency slots open every weekday morning. Call
before 09:00 and we will nearly always see you the same day.

# Prices

A new patient examination including two X-rays is £75. A routine check-up for an
existing patient is £45. Hygiene appointments are £70 for 30 minutes, £95 for
45 minutes.

White fillings start at £160 depending on the size of the cavity. Root canal
treatment is £450 for a front tooth and £650 for a molar. A crown is £750.
Extractions start at £180.

Teeth whitening is £340 for the take-home kit, £550 for in-chair treatment.
Invisalign starts at £2,400 and we quote exactly after a consultation and scan.

We give a written estimate before any treatment begins. Nothing is charged that
you have not agreed to in writing first.

# Payment and insurance

We accept card, bank transfer and cash. For treatment over £500 we offer
interest-free payment over six months through Tabeo, subject to a soft credit
check that does not affect your score.

We are a private practice and do not hold an NHS contract. We can provide
itemised receipts and treatment codes for insurers including Bupa, Denplan,
AXA Health and Vitality. You pay us and claim it back; we do not bill insurers
directly.

Our own membership plan is £22 a month and covers two check-ups, two hygiene
appointments, all X-rays and 20% off any treatment.

# Emergencies and pain

If a tooth has been knocked out, keep it in milk and call us immediately. Do not
scrub the root. Reimplantation within an hour has the best outcome.

For severe swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing, or swelling that
is spreading towards the eye, go to A&E rather than waiting for us.

Out of hours, call 111 for the NHS urgent dental service. Our answerphone
repeats this number.

# Nervous patients

About one in five of our patients tells us they are anxious. We offer longer
first appointments so nothing is rushed, a stop signal you control, and oral
sedation for £120 or IV sedation with a visiting anaesthetist for £395.

You are welcome to book a free ten-minute visit just to see the room and meet
the dentist before committing to anything.

# Children

We see children from age two. Check-ups for children under 16 are £25 when a
parent is a registered patient. Fluoride varnish is included.

We do not treat children under general anaesthetic on site; those cases are
referred to Leeds Dental Institute.

# New patients

To register we need your previous practice details, a list of medications, and
any relevant medical history. Bring a list rather than trying to remember.

Your first visit takes 40 minutes: examination, X-rays, oral cancer screening,
gum assessment, and a written treatment plan with prices before you leave.
