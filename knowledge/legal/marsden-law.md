# About Marsden Law

Marsden Law is a six-solicitor firm in Nottingham. We work in employment law,
family law, wills and probate, and residential conveyancing. We are regulated by
the Solicitors Regulation Authority.

Office hours are Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:30. Appointments outside those
hours can be arranged and are often easier by video.

Nothing on this page is legal advice. It is general information about how the
firm works. Advice requires knowing the facts of your specific matter.

# First consultation

The first 30 minutes is free, by phone, video or in person. In that call we
establish what your matter is, whether we can act for you, and what it is likely
to cost.

We run a conflict check before giving any advice. If we already act for someone
on the other side we have to decline, and we will tell you straight away so you
can go elsewhere without losing time.

To make the first call useful, have the key dates, any contract or letter that
started the problem, and a short timeline of what happened.

# Fees

Our hourly rates are £280 for a partner, £210 for a senior solicitor and £160
for a solicitor. VAT is added to all of these.

Fixed fees are available for defined pieces of work. A single will is £250. Mirror
wills for a couple are £395. Lasting power of attorney is £450 for one, £700 for
both types. Residential conveyancing starts at £1,150 plus disbursements.

Employment settlement agreement advice is usually paid by your employer, most
often between £350 and £750, and we will tell you before starting if their
contribution will not cover it.

We give a written costs estimate before any chargeable work begins, and we tell
you when a matter is heading past that estimate rather than after.

# Employment law

We act mostly for employees: unfair dismissal, discrimination, settlement
agreements, redundancy and grievances.

The tribunal time limit is three months less one day from the act complained of.
It is short and it is strict. Before a claim you must notify ACAS for early
conciliation, which pauses the clock.

Most matters settle. Around 8 in 10 of the employment cases we open resolve by
negotiation or at conciliation without a tribunal hearing.

# Family law

Divorce, financial settlements and arrangements for children.

The court fee for a divorce application is £593, paid to the court rather than
to us. Our fixed fee for an undefended divorce is £750 plus VAT.

Mediation is required before most children and finance applications, apart from
cases involving domestic abuse. We can refer you to a local mediator.

# Wills and probate

A single will is £250 plus VAT. Mirror wills for a couple are £395. A lasting
power of attorney is £450 for one type, £700 for both. These are fixed fees, not
estimates.

Grant of probate only, where the family handles the administration, is £950 plus
VAT. Full estate administration is charged hourly and typically runs 1.5% to 3%
of the estate value.

Uncontested estates usually take six to nine months. Anything involving a
property sale, foreign assets or an inheritance tax return takes longer.

# Conveyancing

Residential purchase from £1,150 plus VAT and disbursements, which are searches,
Land Registry fees and stamp duty. We give a full breakdown before you instruct
us, with nothing hidden as an extra later.

Average time from instruction to completion is currently 12 to 16 weeks. Leasehold
adds three to five weeks because of the management pack.

# Legal aid and what we cannot help with

We do not hold a legal aid contract. If your matter is likely to qualify, we will
tell you and point you at a firm that does.

We do not take criminal, immigration or personal injury work. We will refer you
rather than take on something outside our competence.
