# About Northlight Coaching

Northlight Coaching helps people move from senior individual contributor into
their first leadership role. Two coaches, both former engineering and operations
managers, working with about 40 clients a year.

Everything is remote. Clients are mostly in the UK, Europe and the US east
coast.

# Programmes, prices and what we charge

The Foundations group programme is £1,450. Eight weeks, a 90-minute group
session each week capped at eight people, plus a workbook and a private channel
between sessions.

One-to-one coaching is £220 a session, or £1,900 for a block of ten. Sessions
are 50 minutes, usually fortnightly.

The Intensive is £3,800: twelve one-to-one sessions over six months, a 360
feedback exercise with your colleagues, and email access between sessions.

Payment plans are available on everything over £1,000: three monthly
instalments at no extra cost, or six with a 6% surcharge.

Corporate rates apply when an employer pays for three or more people. Ask and we
will invoice the company directly with a PO if you need one.

# Is it right for me

Foundations suits you if you have been promoted in the last year, or expect to
be in the next six months, and you are finding the shift from doing the work to
getting work done through others harder than expected.

One-to-one suits you if you have a specific situation: a difficult report, a
reorg, a promotion case you keep losing, or a decision about whether to stay on
the management track at all.

If you are looking for interview preparation or a CV rewrite, this is not that.
We can point you at people who do it well.

# How long it takes and the time commitment

Foundations is 90 minutes of live session a week plus about an hour of
exercises. Eight weeks. Sessions are recorded, so missing one is recoverable,
though the group is most of the value.

One-to-one is 50 minutes a fortnight plus whatever you choose to do between
sessions. People who do nothing between sessions get very little out of it, and
we say so before you pay.

# Refunds and cancellations

Foundations: full refund if you ask before the second session. After the second
session, no refund, because the material is delivered.

One-to-one blocks: unused sessions are refunded pro rata at any time, no
questions asked.

Individual sessions can be rescheduled free with 24 hours' notice. Inside 24
hours the session is charged, unless it is an emergency, in which case tell us
and we will not charge you.

# How to start

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute call. It is a real conversation
about your situation, not a sales pitch with a discount at the end.

About one in four of those calls ends with us saying coaching is not what you
need right now. That is a good outcome and it is free.

If we go ahead, you get a written agreement covering scope, schedule, price and
confidentiality before any payment.

# Confidentiality

Sessions are confidential. When an employer pays, they receive confirmation that
sessions happened and nothing about their content, and that is written into the
agreement.

We do not record one-to-one sessions. Group sessions are recorded for
participants only and deleted after 90 days.

# Availability and waitlist

Foundations runs four times a year: January, April, July and October. Cohorts
usually fill four to six weeks ahead.

One-to-one availability varies. When both coaches are full we run a waitlist and
give you an honest date rather than squeezing you in badly.

Ask about current availability and we will tell you what is actually open.
