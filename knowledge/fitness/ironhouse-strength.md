# About Ironhouse Strength

Ironhouse Strength is a strength and conditioning gym in Manchester. Two floors,
eight lifting platforms, a turf area and a small class studio. About 480 active
members.

We are open 05:30 to 22:00 Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 19:00 Saturday and Sunday.
Members get 24-hour fob access from month two.

# Membership prices: how much it costs to join

Off-peak is £39 a month and covers 05:30 to 16:00 on weekdays plus all weekend.
Full membership is £54 a month with no time restrictions.

The unlimited class pass is £72 a month and includes gym access. A ten-class
bundle is £95 and the classes expire after four months.

Students and NHS staff get 15% off with valid ID. Couples pay £96 a month for
two full memberships.

There is a £20 joining fee that covers your induction and your fob. We waive it
if you join on the same day as a trial.

Day passes are £12. A week's trial is £20 and comes off your joining fee if you
sign up.

# Contracts, freezing and cancelling

Memberships are rolling monthly. There is no twelve-month tie-in.

To cancel, give 30 days' notice in writing or through the app. We do not require
a phone call and we will not put you through a retention script.

You can freeze your membership for up to three months a year at £8 a month,
which keeps your joining fee and your rate. Medical freezes are free with a
doctor's note and have no time limit.

# Classes

Barbell Club runs Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 06:15, 12:15 and 18:15.
Conditioning is Tuesday and Thursday at 06:15 and 18:30. Mobility is Sunday at
10:00.

Classes cap at 14 people. Booking opens seven days ahead in the app. If a class
is full, join the waitlist; roughly a third of waitlisted members get in.

Missing a booked class without cancelling four hours ahead costs a £5 no-show
charge. It exists because a full waitlist means somebody else wanted that spot.

# Personal training

Six coaches, all with a strength background rather than a general fitness
qualification only.

One-to-one sessions are £45, or £400 for a block of ten. Semi-private with two
or three people is £28 each per session.

Every new member gets one free 45-minute session covering technique on the main
lifts and a starting programme. It is a genuine session, not a sales meeting.

Online programming without in-person sessions is £90 a month.

# Facilities

Eight competition platforms with Eleiko bars and bumpers, three squat racks, a
full dumbbell rack to 50 kg, two sled tracks, assault bikes, rowers and a
Concept2 SkiErg.

Changing rooms have showers, lockers and a drying room. Bring your own padlock
or buy one at reception for £4.

Parking is free for two hours in the retail park next door. There is secure bike
storage inside the entrance.

# Bringing a guest

Members can bring a guest twice a month free. Additional guests are £8. Guests
must be over 16 and complete a health form at reception.

Under-18s can train with a parent present after an induction with a coach.

# Corporate and company rates

We run corporate rates for companies within two miles: 20% off full membership
from five employees, 30% off from fifteen. It is invoiced monthly to the company
rather than to individuals.

Several local employers cover part of the cost as a benefit. We can provide a
one-page proposal for your HR team.
