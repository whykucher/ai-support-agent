# About Halyard Digital

Halyard Digital is a performance marketing agency of eleven people. We run paid
search, paid social, SEO and lifecycle email, mostly for e-commerce brands and
B2B companies doing between £1m and £30m a year.

We take on about two new clients a month and deliberately cap the roster at
eighteen, because past that the senior people stop touching the accounts.

# What we do and what we do not

We do: Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, technical and content SEO, Klaviyo
and HubSpot lifecycle email, landing pages, analytics and attribution setup.

We do not: brand identity, PR, video production, or building your website from
scratch. We work alongside the people who do, and we will introduce you.

We do not white-label for other agencies. Every client is direct.

# Pricing: what we charge and our retainers

We manage paid media - Google Ads, Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn - for 12% of ad
spend, with a £2,400 monthly minimum. Below about £20k a month in spend the
percentage stops making sense and we quote a flat fee instead.

SEO retainers start at £3,200 a month. Lifecycle email is £2,000 a month, or
£3,500 bundled with paid media.

A standalone audit is £4,500 and takes three weeks. It is a fixed fee, you keep
the document, and there is no obligation to retain us afterwards. Roughly half
of audit clients do.

Minimum retainer term is three months, then rolling with 30 days' notice. The
three months exist because nothing meaningful can be judged in six weeks, not
because we want to trap anyone.

To cancel, email your strategist. Notice runs from that email, and we hand over
everything during the notice period rather than switching off on day one.
Months already worked are not refunded; the audit fee is refundable in full if
you cancel before we start work on it.

# Onboarding

Week one is access and audit: ad accounts, analytics, tag manager, CRM, email
platform. You keep ownership of every account; we work inside yours, never ours.

Week two is the plan: the channels we will run, the budget split, the target
CPA or ROAS, and what we will stop doing.

Week three is launch. Most accounts see the first structural changes live inside
fifteen working days of signing.

# Reporting

You get a live dashboard from day one, not a monthly PDF. It updates daily and
you can look at it whenever you want without asking us.

We hold a 45-minute call every fortnight, and a longer strategy review each
quarter. If a campaign breaks, you hear it from us before you notice it.

Attribution is a genuine argument in this industry. We report platform-reported
numbers and your own back-end revenue side by side, and we tell you when they
disagree rather than quoting whichever is flattering.

# Results and expectations

Paid media usually shows a direction inside four to six weeks. SEO is six to
nine months before it is worth judging, and anyone who promises faster is
selling you something.

We will not guarantee a specific ROAS or ranking. Any agency that does is either
inexperienced or lying, and both are expensive.

What we will commit to is a defined scope, a reporting cadence, and telling you
early when something is not working.

# Ad spend and billing

Ad spend goes on your own cards, billed by the platforms directly to you. We
never mark up media. Our invoice is management fee only.

Invoices are issued on the 1st, payable in 14 days. Everything is on a written
statement of work; we do not start on a verbal go-ahead.

# Who we are a bad fit for

Brands spending under about £8,000 a month on media. The fee is a large share of
your budget at that level and you are better off in-house for now.

Anyone who needs approval from four people before a headline can change. Paid
media dies at that speed.

Businesses that want us to also fix the product, the pricing and the site. We
can tell you those are the problem, but marketing will not outrun them.
