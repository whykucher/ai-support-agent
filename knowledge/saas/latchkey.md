# About Latchkey

Latchkey is scheduling and dispatch software for field service teams: plumbers,
electricians, HVAC, pest control, anyone sending vans to jobs. About 1,400
companies use it, mostly between five and eighty engineers.

We are a team of nineteen, based in Bristol and remote across Europe.

# Plans, pricing and what we charge

Starter is $29 per user per month: scheduling, job cards, customer records and
the mobile app. Minimum three users.

Growth is $49 per user per month and adds invoicing, parts and stock, recurring
jobs, custom job forms and the customer portal.

Scale is $79 per user per month and adds the API, SSO, custom roles, audit logs
and a named account manager.

Annual billing is two months free. Prices are per active user; you can
deactivate a seat any time and it stops billing on the next cycle.

So a ten-engineer team on Growth costs $490 a month, or $4,900 a year on annual
billing. There is nothing else to pay: no per-job fee, no SMS surcharge and no
charge for the mobile app.

There is no setup fee and no minimum contract on monthly plans.

# Free trial and getting started

To get started, start the trial from the site: fourteen days, all Growth
features, no card required. The trial does not
downgrade at the end, it stops, so nothing surprises you.

You can import your existing customers and jobs during the trial and keep that
data if you subscribe. If you do not subscribe, we delete it after 30 days.

Trials can be extended once by a week if you ask. Most people who ask are mid
migration and we would rather they finished properly.

# Integrations

Native: QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, GoCardless, Google Calendar, Outlook, Twilio,
Mailchimp and Zapier.

The REST API is available on Scale, and there is a webhook system on Growth and
above that fires on job created, job completed, invoice paid and engineer
assigned. Rate limit is 120 requests a minute on Scale.

We do not have a native Salesforce app. Customers who need it use the API or
Zapier, and we will help you scope it.

# Data, security and hosting

Data is hosted in the EU on AWS, in eu-west-1, with daily encrypted backups
retained 35 days. Encrypted in transit and at rest.

We are ISO 27001 certified and complete SOC 2 Type II annually. We will send the
report under NDA. A security questionnaire from your team goes to
security@latchkey.example and gets a reply within three working days.

You can export everything you have put in, at any time, as CSV or through the
API. Your data is yours and there is no export fee.

# Migration and onboarding

Growth and Scale include a guided migration: we take your spreadsheets or an
export from your current system and load customers, sites, job history and
recurring schedules.

Typical migration takes five to ten working days depending on how clean the
data is. Cleaning it is usually the slow part, not loading it.

Onboarding includes two training sessions for office staff and one for
engineers, plus recorded walkthroughs your new starters can use later.

# Support

Email and in-app chat, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00 UK. Median first response
is under two hours during those hours.

Scale customers get a phone line and a four-hour response target, including a
two-hour target for anything that stops jobs being dispatched.

Documentation and the API reference are public at docs.latchkey.example.

# Cancelling and changing plan

Cancel from the billing page any time. Monthly plans stop at the end of the
current period; there are no cancellation fees and nobody will phone you to talk
you out of it.

Annual plans can be cancelled but are not refunded pro rata, except within the
first 30 days, when we refund in full.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the
next renewal so you do not lose features mid-month.

# Enterprise

Above about 80 engineers we quote directly rather than per seat, and that
usually includes SSO, a custom SLA, a sandbox environment and quarterly reviews.

Procurement, DPAs, custom contract terms and security reviews go through
sales@latchkey.example. Expect two weeks end to end, faster if your legal team
is quick.
