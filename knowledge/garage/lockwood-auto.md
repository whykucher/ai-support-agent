# About Lockwood Auto

Lockwood Auto is an independent garage in Sheffield with six ramps and eight
technicians. We service and repair all makes, and we are an approved MOT test
centre for classes 4 and 7.

Open Monday to Friday 07:30 to 18:00 and Saturday 08:00 to 13:00. Closed Sunday.

# Servicing and prices

An interim service is £129 and covers oil, oil filter, and a 30-point check.
A full service is £229 and adds air filter, cabin filter, brake fluid test and a
60-point check. A major service with spark plugs and fuel filter is £389.

An MOT is £54.85, the legal maximum. Booked with any service it is £35.

Diagnostics are £60 for the first hour, which we credit against the repair if
you go ahead with the work here.

Air conditioning regas is £89 for R134a and £149 for R1234yf, which is what most
cars built after 2017 use.

# Quotes and approvals

You get a written quote before any work starts. If we find something else once
the car is on the ramp, we stop, photograph it, send you the photo and a price,
and wait. We do not carry out unapproved work and then invoice for it.

Quotes are valid for 14 days. Parts prices move, labour does not.

Our labour rate is £68 an hour plus VAT, which is roughly half main-dealer rate
in this city.

# Warranty and parts

All repairs carry a 12-month or 12,000-mile parts and labour warranty, whichever
comes first.

Servicing here does not void a manufacturer warranty. Block Exemption
Regulation means an independent garage using matching-quality parts and keeping
records is entitled to service a car under warranty. We stamp the book and
record it digitally.

We fit OE or OE-equivalent parts as standard. If you want genuine manufacturer
parts specifically, say so and we will price them; usually 25 to 60% more.

# Booking and turnaround

Routine servicing books about a week ahead. MOTs usually have space within three
days. Book online, by phone or by dropping in.

Most services are same-day if the car is in before 09:00. Diagnostics are
same-day. Larger jobs like a clutch or a cambelt are one to two days.

Free courtesy cars are available on servicing and MOT bookings, subject to
availability and a valid licence with no more than six points. Book it when you
book the car in; we have four.

We also do free local collection and delivery within five miles.

# MOT failures

If your car fails, we send you the failure sheet with photographs and a priced
repair quote. You are under no obligation to have the work done here.

A partial retest within ten working days is free if the car stays with us, or
£27 if it leaves and comes back.

Advisories are not failures. We will tell you plainly which advisories matter
now and which can wait until the next service.

# Fleet and business customers

We look after around 40 local vans and company cars. Fleet accounts get priority
booking, 10% off labour, 30-day invoicing and a monthly report of spend per
vehicle.

Minimum for a fleet account is three vehicles. Ask for a fleet quote and we will
put numbers to your actual list.

# What we do not do

We do not do bodywork, paint or windscreens; we will point you at people we
trust. We do not work on HGVs. We do not currently service electric vehicles
beyond tyres, brakes and MOT, though our high-voltage training is booked.
